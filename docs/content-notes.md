# Content decisions

The 37 files in `raw messy data/` are the primary source of truth. Every file was scanned, and all 364 distinct media URLs were downloaded successfully. The sitemap repeats page content and image descriptions; it was treated as corroborating crawl data, not a separate source of new business claims.

- The date of implementation is September 25, 2026. Only the twelve journeys following that date are in the upcoming collection. Greece (September 14–24), Croatia, Cuba, Cartagena, and the Goshen Farms event are historical, not upcoming.
- Turkey’s homepage says October 28–November 16; its event page and calendar agree on October 28–November 6, 2026. The latter is used. The source calls it both a 10-day and 11-day journey, so the site omits that duration claim.
- Croatia’s calendar start differs from the prose date. It is not included as an upcoming trip.
- The Cuba Jazz index text contains Habano Festival dates, but its event page describes a different festival. Neither is advertised as upcoming.
- Spain/Morocco and Brazil application links point to a missing store page. Ireland, South Korea, and Vietnam/Cambodia application links point back to themselves. All inquiry CTAs now use the genuine existing contact form. No checkout or payment flow is invented.
- No source email, phone number, or postal address was found. None is displayed.
- The about page states “over 1,200 GrooveTrippers.” This is the source of the community figure. There are no fabricated review scores, testimonials, destination counts, or operating-history claims.
- No live booking availability was verified. The site does not claim trips are currently available or sold out and directs travelers to the team for confirmation and pricing.
- The Travel Savings Account and matching concepts appear in the about copy, but no working APIs or product implementation were supplied. No pretend financial account, matching feature, or unsupported signup workflow was created.
- The Bhutan article has an Adriana Marcano byline and a Jannelle signature. The source byline and original story are preserved rather than silently resolving that ambiguity.
- The Cuba article title includes a stray “June 16,2023”; the consistent June 4, 2026 journal date is retained, and the stray date is removed from its title.
- The founder quote on the homepage is an excerpt from the Japan story. It is attributed to Jannelle.
- Trip imagery is original source imagery. The 2026 Bali and Turkey card images use the respective homepage destination images. All images are locally served; source URLs exist only in provenance records and outbound text links.
- Editorial headings describe the supplied travel concept; they are not additional business facts.
