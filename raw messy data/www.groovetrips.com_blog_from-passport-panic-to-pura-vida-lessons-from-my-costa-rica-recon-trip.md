# From Passport Panic to Pura Vida: Lessons from My Costa Rica Recon Trip

Aug 5

Written By [Jannelle Cortes](https://www.groovetrips.com/blog?author=5a75353d5e0ed86ea6f814b2)

Travel is equal parts magic and unpredictability. No matter how seasoned of a traveler you are, sometimes the universe throws you a curveball just to test your wits.

My recent trip to Costa Rica was meant to be a simple reconnaissance mission - a chance to scope out gorgeous spots for future wellness retreats. Instead, it kicked off with an airport nightmare, ended with sunset beach bliss, and taught me a few unforgettable travel lessons along the way.

Here is what went down, how I survived two unexpected days in Houston, and the hidden gems of Guanacaste, Costa Rica.

#### No Good Deed Goes Unpunished

I consider myself pretty vigilant when traveling. My passport is usually glued to my person. But during my connection, I sat near a fellow traveler who was aggressively sneezing - gushing snot everywhere. Feeling sympathetic, I reached into my bag to hand him some tissues.

What I _didn't_ realize was that while pulling out the tissues, my passport caught on something and slid right out onto my seat.

Fast forward to the gate for my international flight to Liberia, Costa Rica: **Panic.** No passport. After reporting it missing and hitting a brick wall at airport lost-and-found, I was officially stuck in Houston. To make matters worse, the Houston passport office only handles emergency walk-ins from **7:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m.** Since I had already missed that tiny three-hour window, I had to wait until the next morning.

**Travel Reality Check:** Even in 2026, government bureaucracy moves at its own pace. You still need physical, printed paper documents for an emergency passport - digital backups won't suffice.

Luckily, my amazing "shimmy sister," Andriette, came to the ultimate rescue! I crashed with her for two nights while she helped me track down a printer, sign endless documents, and get my life in order. It was a crazy, chaotic 48 hours, but getting quality time with her was the silver lining I didn't know I needed.

Two days late, new emergency passport in hand, I was finally on a plane to Costa Rica.

#### Las Catalinas - Luxury, Black Sands & Sticker Shock

Landing in Liberia was instant relief. Even though it was the beginning of the dry season, a gentle rain fell as we stepped off the plane, and that warm, tropical humidity hit immediately. After about an hour’s drive, we arrived at **Las Catalinas**.

Las Catalinas is a stunning, highly manicured, colorful private community. It definitely feels like an expat enclave - a bit detached from local Costa Rican culture - but after two days of airport bureaucracy, that quiet, pampering bubble was _exactly_ what I needed.

**Highlights:**

- **The Beach:** A breathtaking private stretch of black sand meeting crystal-clear water with absolutely zero trash or debris in sight.

- **Accommodations:** Top-notch, serene, and ultra-relaxing.


**The Catch:**

Costa Rica is **_expensive_**. I don't know if it's the post-pandemic tourism boom or the location, but paying **$20 for a coffee and smoothie** or **$26 for a basic omelet** had my jaw on the floor.

**Pro Tip: Eat at the _Sodas_**

If you want authentic, mouthwatering food without the exorbitant resort markups, look for roadside **sodas**. These are small, local, family-run spots where the locals actually eat. The food is significantly cheaper and packs way more traditional flavor than high-end resort menus.

#### Tamarindo Sails & Sweet Tree Treehouse Magic

Before heading to our next stay, we made a pitstop in the Tamarindo area for a catamaran tour. **Tamarindo** is definitely party central—if you’re looking for nightlife, surf, and high-energy beach bars, that’s your spot. I was personally glad to be staying somewhere quieter, but the sailing trip was a highlight!

Next, we checked into the **Sweet Tree Treehouse Resort**. **Important Booking Tip:** Make sure you explicitly request an actual _treehouse_ and not a bungalow! (We made that mix-up initially, but learned for next time.)

This place is a lush, peaceful sanctuary hidden in the canopy. They have a stunning overlook lounge with a **glass floor** (if you have a fear of heights, word of advice: _don't look down!_), three gorgeous swimming pools, and some treehouses even feature their own private plunge pools.

Hands down, **the best food of the entire trip was right here**. The culinary team put out five-star, exquisite meals. We met a couple staying there for five full nights, and I could instantly see why. It’s super close to local eco-activities, making it the ultimate balance of adventure and luxury.

#### Nosara - Finding the Sweet Spot

Our final leg took us to **Nosara**, and it immediately stole my heart.

If Las Catalinas is ultra-manicured luxury and Tamarindo is a wild party, Nosara is the perfect middle ground. It’s a bohemian surfer town that feels laid-back, grounded, and authentically charming.

**Top Spots in Nosara:**

- **Sunset at La Luna:** We caught an incredible sunset right on the beach at _La Luna -_ watching the sun dip into the ocean while dining was magical.

- **Astra Hotel Boutique:** We stayed at Astra, an intimate 8-room boutique property. It felt incredibly private, and the staff went above and beyond to make us feel welcome. Easy 3-to-4-star luxury with 5-star hospitality.

- **Bodhi Tree:** I toured the _Bodhi Village / Bodhi Tree Yoga Resort_, which is an absolute dreamland for anyone into yoga, wellness, and mindful living.


#### The Takeaway & What’s Next?

This trip started as a lesson in keeping your wits about you when travel plans go off the rails, but it ended up being the perfect scout mission.

Costa Rica’s lush jungles, warm ocean breezes, and deep-rooted _Pura Vida_ spirit left a lasting impression. I walked away knowing exactly where I want to bring my community next.
