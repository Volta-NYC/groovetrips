#### Thailand: Bangkok, Phuket, Chiang Mai

**November 8-18, 2026**

This is Southeast Asia at its most vibrant, **_where ancient traditions meet bustling modern life, misty mountain peaks, and sun-soaked paradise._**

You begin in **Bangkok**, the electrifying heart of the kingdom, where golden temples line the Chao Phraya River and neon-lit street markets awaken as the sun goes down. Wander through bustling night bazaars, sample world-renowned street food, and feel the undeniable pulse of the city.

From the city's energy, escape to the tranquil north of **Chiang Mai**. Tucked into misty mountain valleys, this cultural sanctuary invites you to explore ancient temples, discover serene river views, and experience a slower, richer rhythm of life.

Finally, **Phuket** awaits to wash your cares away. Unwind on powder-soft beaches framed by turquoise waters as the sun dips into the Andaman Sea. From island breezes to unforgettable sunsets, this is a journey designed to restore, inspire, and captivate.

This isn’t just a tropical getaway. It’s a journey through rich history, warm Thai hospitality, and landscapes you’ll never forget.
