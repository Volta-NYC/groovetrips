#### Happiness, Heritage & Himalayan Beauty Bhutan

#### February 28-March 8, 2027

Bhutan is known as the “Land of Happiness”, it’s a Buddhist country where the government actually measures Gross National Happiness. It’s deeply nature-oriented (perfect for hikers and wellness folks) and ideal for travel aficionados looking for something truly unique. I absolutely loved my time there; the people are incredibly warm, and the treatment makes you feel like absolute royalty.
