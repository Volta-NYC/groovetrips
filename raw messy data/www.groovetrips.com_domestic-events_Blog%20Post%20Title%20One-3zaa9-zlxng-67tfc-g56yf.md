# Day of Play at Goshen Farms \| Aug 13, 2026

Jul 1

Written By [Jannelle Cortes](https://www.groovetrips.com/domestic-events?author=5a75353d5e0ed86ea6f814b2)

### **Join Us for a Day of Play at Goshen Farms!**

We are scheduling another incredible **Day of Play** designed specifically for entrepreneurs, creators, visionaries, friends, and families to unplug, recharge, and connect.

### **Event Details**

- **When:** Aug 13, 2026

- **Where:** Goshen Farms (Beautiful grounds owned by artist Susan Hillary)

- **Fuel for the Day:** Organic snacks and lunch are fully provided!


### **What’s on the Agenda?**

- **Center Yourself:** Grounding meditation and restorative yoga sessions.

- **Move Your Body:** Expressive West African Dance and Belly Dancing.

- **The Pool is Open!** Take a refreshing dip, lounge by the water, and soak up the summer sun. _(Note: You might want to update this description on the site, as the flyer copy accidentally repeated the dance description here!)_

- **Get Dirty:** Hands-on planting in the rich farm soil.

- **Get Inspired:** A curated art walk through the farm's stunning, scenic grounds.

- **Connect:** Authentic, intentional networking with fellow visionaries and like-minded community members.

- **Shop Local:** Browse and buy farm-fresh local delicacies, including jams, honey, artisanal teas, cheeses, and meats.
