#### **Greece:****Where antiquity meets the Aegean.**

**September 14-24**—**2026**

This eleven-day odyssey is a masterclass in balance, weaving together the triumvirate of Greek excellence: history, hedonism, and hospitality. It begins in the cradle of democracy, where you will toast the Acropolis by twilight from a hidden rooftop bar before diving into the vibrant plate-smashing energy of a beachside nightclub. The journey then leaps across the Aegean to the whitewashed labyrinth of Mykonos, where days are spent with the wind in your hair, whether you are racing an ATV along the coast to Ornos or learning the secrets of phyllo and olive oil in a traditional home kitchen.

The grand finale unfolds in Santorini, where your cliffside perch offers front-row seats to the world’s most famous sunset. Here, you will sip Assyrtiko among the vines, feast on freshly caught shrimp as you sail into the volcanic caldera, and succumb entirely to the deep, satisfying rhythm of island life. This is Greece stripped of guesswork and wrapped in luxury, leaving you with nothing to do but simply arrive and be swept away.
