# Meet The CEO

![A woman with curly dark hair, smiling with pink lipstick, wearing a black top, against a light background.](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/6924ca09a30eb459ab5a3e05/3a531cc1-daf4-4c70-bf2e-3d9a8717bd7a/Jannelle+Headshot.jpeg)

**Jannelle Cortes**

_Founder, GrooveTrips \| Choreographer \| Travel Advocate_

Jannelle is a second-generation travel professional and first-generation Dominican-American entrepreneur who has masterfully woven together her two lifelong passions: dance and travel. After a 20-year career as a choreographer in New York City, she founded GrooveTrips to create immersive, culturally rich travel experiences for artists and adventurers.

**Her Mission:**

To make travel experiences accessible, meaningful, and beneficial for both travelers and the communities they visit. Through GrooveTrips, she designs and leads immersive journeys around the world, collaborating with local vendors and wellness professionals to create culturally respectful, sustainable, and transformative travel experiences.

Jannelle is also a proud Brand Ambassador for Capezio in Cuba and a correspondent for CaribbeanOneTV, using her platform to amplify diverse voices across the travel and arts industries.

# Our Mission

At GrooveTrips, we empower travelers to explore the world meaningfully—alongside their Travel Tribe. We match you with like-minded travel companions using personalized profiles, while making journeys more affordable and accessible with our Travel Savings Account (TSA). We are committed to cultural immersion and sustainability by partnering directly with local artists, guides, and vendors.

# Our Vision

We are reimagining travel for the modern explorer. Our vision is a world where travel is personalized, accessible, and community-driven—replacing impersonal bookings with curated group journeys that connect people, support local economies, and celebrate diverse cultures.

# Who We Are

At GrooveTrips, we believe travel is about connection, where your vibe truly meets your tribe!

We are a vibrant travel community dedicated to crafting immersive, sustainable, and accessible experiences for all. Our unique approach allows you to book both air travel and tours in one convenient place, ensuring every adventure is seamless.

What sets us apart is our commitment to local communities. We partner with vetted small tour operators and trusted properties, creating a once-stop shop for travelers. Our growing community of over 1,200 GrooveTrippers means more opportunities to find the perfect trip that resonates with your travel style.
